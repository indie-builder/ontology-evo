# Third-party references

This project integrates with [EvoOntology](https://github.com/ruc-datalab/EvoOntology) through its official Claude Code plugin. EvoOntology is distributed under the MIT license. Its runtime is installed separately and is not vendored in this repository.

The ontology examples, synthetic sales fixture and workspace instructions in this repository are project-specific integration material. They do not imply endorsement by the EvoOntology authors or Anthropic.

The optional offline ontology explorer is generated locally by EvoOntology and is not committed. Refer to the installed plugin and generated artifact for their bundled dependency notices.
